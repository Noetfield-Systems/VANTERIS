# Commercial operations v0.5
Operator /operator/finance issues milestone payment requests against accepted proposals. The database serializes issuance by locking the proposal; total non-void invoices cannot exceed accepted scope. Seller identity is required. Client portal queries invoices by authenticated lead; checkout uses server-owned amount and currency; one order per invoice and provider idempotency key. Signed webhook marks order and invoice paid in one transaction. Expired sessions currently require support; live checkout, provider fee/payout reconciliation, chargebacks, refunds, tax invoice compliance and agreement signing still require implementation and testing. The invoice here is an initial payment request record, not a claim of tax-compliant invoice issuance. Confirm seller-specific legal and tax fields before live use. Expenses distinguish internal vs external costs; dashboard totals reflect paid orders and recorded costs, not recognized revenue or complete bookkeeping. Intelligence operator records source, publisher, publication date, retrieval, verification, owner and next action; no automatic external research scheduler is implemented.
