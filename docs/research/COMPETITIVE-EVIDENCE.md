## Follow-through
Use the 15 references as a research and procurement matrix, not a list of signed partners. Benchmark actual customer tasks: select appropriate service, understand scope and fees, request discovery, book slot, accept proposal, pay, access project, request support. Capture exact URL, date, device, screenshots, steps, completion and friction. For technology vendors, test sandbox features only with authorization. Procurement requires eligibility, documented controls, integration cost, service terms and exit options. No numerical parity score until evidence exists.
