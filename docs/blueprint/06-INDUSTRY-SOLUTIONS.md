# Industry solution blueprints
For each sector use activity map → operating model → market and specialist questions → vendor comparison → implementation → acceptance. Exchange: custody, matching, reconciliation. Forex: liquidity, pricing, platform and risk. Payments: flow, settlement and provider boundaries. Digital banks: ledger and partner orchestration. Wallets: custody and recovery. Tokenization: asset rights and transfer lifecycle. Web3: wallet integration and user acquisition. Blockchain: protocol interface and reliability. Gaming: platform, payments and jurisdiction review. Investment technology: execution boundaries and data. SMEs: entity and market entry. Enterprise: integration, procurement and service levels. Each journey ends in scoped consultation; no regulated product or license is supplied by the website.
