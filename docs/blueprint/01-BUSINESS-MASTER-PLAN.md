# Business master plan
Position: independent international fintech business launch and technical advisory. Customer segments: founders preparing launch, existing operators expanding, enterprise teams procuring integrations. Start with technical consultation, roadmap, market research and architecture assessment; follow with project implementation and recurring operating support. Corporate, regulated and professional work is coordinated through verified specialists under distinct engagement terms.
Acquisition: targeted founder outreach, sector guides, provider referrals, discovery. Conversion: real lead, qualification, scope, proposal, agreement, invoice, checkout, verified receipt, delivery, renewal. Initial objectives (targets not results): 200 researched prospects, 30 candidate partners, 10 qualified conversations, 3 proposals, first collected revenue. Measure weekly: qualified-to-meeting, meeting-to-proposal, proposal-to-paid, delivery margin, time-to-first-response, renewal. Keep independent merchant, accounts, contracts, CRM and data. EXORYQ is only a potential vendor; repository hosting is not legal ownership.
