# Operating structure and autonomous work packages
Founder/Managing Director sets strategy and legal entity. BD qualifies and owns next action; advisory defines scope; corporate and regulatory coordinators hand off to qualified providers; engineering implements; research verifies evidence; partner owner checks credentials and agreements; delivery owns milestones; finance reconciles; success manages support and renewal. Initial staff may cover several roles; no named fictional employees. A brand/product, B research/docs, C CRM/revenue, D portal, E partners, F intelligence/content, G infrastructure, H QA. Every work package: objective, owner role, input, artifact, test, receipt, dependency, next action. Routine: identify → research → prioritize → implement → test → ship → verify → repair → economic activation. Do not claim autonomous schedule until a runtime is deployed.
