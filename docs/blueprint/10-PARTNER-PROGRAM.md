# Partner handbook
Categories: counsel, corporate services, compliance, banks, PSPs, custody, tax/accounting, technology. Candidate fields: public source, country, specialization, qualification evidence, contact, status, terms, owner, next action. Pipeline Candidate → researched → contacted → diligence → agreement → active → paused. Check professional registry where relevant, scope, geography, references, privacy handling and fees. Commercial referral terms require written agreement and disclosure where applicable. No logo or official partner claim without consent. Target 30 researched candidates; no fabricated records.
