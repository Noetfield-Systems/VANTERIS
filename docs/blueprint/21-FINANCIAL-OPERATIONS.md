# Financial operations
Separate books and merchant identity; record proposal, invoice, order, provider transaction, verified receipt, refund, direct cost and external expense. Reconcile gross payment, provider fee, net payout, currency and tax. Pending checkout is not revenue; paid order is verified collection, not automatically recognized revenue; recognize according to applicable accounting rules and delivery. Refunds require provider reconciliation. Track aged receivables, cash collected, gross margin, unbilled work. No shared account unless founder explicitly designates legal seller. Accountant review for applicable tax.
