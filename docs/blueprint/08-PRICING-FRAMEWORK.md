# Pricing and economics
Working prices CAD: consultation 149, roadmap 750, market research 550, architecture 950; validate with actual clients. Scope-specific engineering and managed operations use milestone or retainer terms. Estimate internal labor hours × loaded cost + vendor cost + contingency; contribution margin=(net revenue-direct cost)/net revenue. Quote taxes separately where applicable. Pass-through costs separately disclosed; do not mark up without terms. Before charging: identify actual seller, deliverable, acceptance, timing and refund terms. Never count test payments, pending invoices or proposals as collected revenue.
