# Jurisdiction research handbook
18 markets: Canada, US, UK, UAE, Singapore, Hong Kong, Switzerland, Estonia, Lithuania, Malta, Cyprus, Poland, Germany, Australia, Cayman Islands, BVI, Mauritius, Seychelles. Source seeds in lib/catalog.mjs; not verified legal dossiers. For each create source record: official URL, publisher, retrieval date, effective date, quotation/paraphrase, activity and jurisdiction, reviewer, confidence and next review. Research entity types, registrar, relevant regulators, permissions, ownership, managers, capital, restrictions, official fees and local provider candidates. Mark unknown when not verified. Distinguish national, state/provincial, free zone and activity regimes. Publish only supported findings and refer legal determinations to qualified local professionals.
