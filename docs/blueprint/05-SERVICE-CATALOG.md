# Service delivery catalog
## corporate
Target: Founders, cross-border SMEs. Problem: Unclear legal entity and operating dependencies. Deliverable: Entity type comparison; incorporation task list; specialist brief. Inputs: Ownership, operations, target markets, management. Specialist: Local corporate lawyer/provider. Schedule: 3–7 business days. Pricing: Fixed research fee; third-party quote. External: Registry fees, registered office, counsel. Cross-sell: Expansion, banking. Acceptance: Evidence-linked comparison and named outstanding questions.

## expansion
Target: Operating SMEs and fintechs. Problem: Unclear entry sequence and local dependencies. Deliverable: Entry plan; risk register; operating cost assumptions. Inputs: Current operations, target market, volumes. Specialist: Local corporate and tax advisers. Schedule: 5–10 days. Pricing: Fixed scope. External: Local counsel, filings. Cross-sell: Market research, operations. Acceptance: Prioritized roadmap with owners.

## fintech
Target: Fintech founders. Problem: Unsequenced product, regulatory and funding dependencies. Deliverable: Business launch roadmap; funds-flow diagram; backlog. Inputs: Product, target users, geography, budget. Specialist: Fintech architect; counsel separately. Schedule: 5 days. Pricing: CAD 750 initial roadmap. External: Specialist opinions, vendor fees. Cross-sell: Architecture, engineering. Acceptance: Scope, sequence and dependencies agreed.

## regulatory
Target: Financial product founders. Problem: Unclear licensing questions. Deliverable: Source register; activity map; questions for counsel. Inputs: Activities, custody, funds flow, jurisdictions. Specialist: Qualified local counsel. Schedule: 5–10 days. Pricing: Research fee plus specialist quote. External: Legal fees, application fees. Cross-sell: Corporate, AML. Acceptance: Primary sources with dates; no legal conclusion.

## banking
Target: International businesses. Problem: Incomplete provider readiness information. Deliverable: Readiness checklist; evidence gaps; provider criteria. Inputs: Entity, activity, ownership, volumes, markets. Specialist: Bank/EMI onboarding team. Schedule: 3–7 days. Pricing: Fixed research scope. External: Provider and specialist charges. Cross-sell: Payments, AML. Acceptance: Requirements mapped; no approval guarantee.

## payments
Target: PSPs, merchants, fintechs. Problem: Fragmented payment architecture. Deliverable: Funds-flow design; vendor matrix; integration plan. Inputs: Currencies, transaction volumes, settlement needs. Specialist: Payment engineer; licensed providers. Schedule: 5–10 days. Pricing: Fixed scope or implementation. External: PSP processing and FX fees. Cross-sell: Engineering, AML. Acceptance: Documented flow and failure modes.

## crypto

Specialist: Blockchain architect; counsel. Schedule: 5–10 days. Pricing: Fixed scope. External: Network, custody, legal fees. Cross-sell: Exchange, AML. Acceptance: Threat model and integration dependencies.

## model

Specialist: Financial analyst; accountant if needed. Schedule: 5–10 days. Pricing: Fixed scope. External: Accounting or specialist review. Cross-sell: Roadmap, operations. Acceptance: Editable model and reconciled assumptions.

## engineering
Target: Fintech product teams. Problem: Missing implementation capacity. Deliverable: Agreed software, documentation and tests. Inputs: Requirements, access, security constraints. Specialist: Software engineer. Schedule: Milestone-dependent. Pricing: Fixed milestone or time and materials. External: Cloud, APIs, licenses. Cross-sell: Managed operations. Acceptance: Acceptance tests and handover.

## operations
Target: Operating businesses. Problem: Unowned operational tasks. Deliverable: Milestone tracker; delivery reviews; runbook. Inputs: Workload, service scope, dependencies. Specialist: Project manager; specialists. Schedule: Monthly. Pricing: Retainer with capacity limits. External: External service fees. Cross-sell: Enterprise advisory. Acceptance: Tracked completion and exceptions.

## enterprise
Target: Financial institutions. Problem: Complex multi-vendor delivery. Deliverable: Program scope; governance and dependency map. Inputs: Stakeholders, architecture, constraints. Specialist: Program lead and technical specialists. Schedule: 2–4 weeks discovery. Pricing: Custom proposal. External: Vendor and specialist costs. Cross-sell: Engineering, operations. Acceptance: Signed scope and measurable milestones.
