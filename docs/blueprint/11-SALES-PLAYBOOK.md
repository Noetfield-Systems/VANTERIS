# Sales playbook
ICP: founders launching fintech with defined activity and geography; operators with integration needs; international SMEs expanding. Qualification: project, urgency, budget range, authority, geography, feasible deliverable. Research public business information; record source, date, trigger, relevance, channel and applicable outreach rules. Personalized message: specific public business context, relevant observation, question, optional discovery invitation, identity and opt-out as required. No invented replies. Pipeline stages in catalog; set owner, next action and deadline. Follow-up only where permitted; stop on opt-out. Weekly review: actual replies, meetings, proposals, receipts, reasons lost.
