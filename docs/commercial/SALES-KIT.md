# VANTERIS | Sales and go-to-market kit
## Positioning
Independent fintech business launch research, market entry planning, architecture and project delivery. We turn complex decisions into defined workstreams. Not a law firm, broker, bank, investment adviser or licensing authority; reserved work requires separately qualified professionals.
## Offer ladder
Discovery request → CAD 149 decision session → CAD 550 market research / CAD 750 launch roadmap / CAD 950 architecture review → scoped implementation or managed delivery. Published prices are existing CAD service fees; confirm tax, external fees, capacity, terms and dates before accepting payment. No invented discounts, testimonials or accreditation.
## Ideal customer
1. Fintech founder with active launch plans, defined product, target market and budget owner. Lead with roadmap.
2. Existing fintech/payment business entering a new market. Lead with two-market research.
3. CTO/product lead selecting or integrating financial technology. Lead with architecture review.
4. Owner with a specific business decision. Lead with consultation.
## Discovery questions
What decision must be made? Which product, jurisdiction and customers? What is already in place? What must be delivered and by when? Who owns budget and approval? What external professional input is required? What outcome is explicitly out of scope?
## Proposal template
Client and actual contracting seller; current context; objectives and deliverables; agreed inputs and exclusions; milestone dates and named owners; fee, taxes, third-party costs, payment schedule; change control; acceptance criteria; confidentiality and data handling; cancellation/refund terms; signature or recorded acceptance. Use actual staff availability. Do not promise licensing, bank accounts, investment or approvals.

## Sales objections
- "Can you guarantee a licence or banking?" No. We can map research and technical requirements; qualified providers advise separately.
- "Why a fixed scope?" It gives a concrete decision output before a broader engagement.
- "Can you implement?" Yes, subject to agreed scope, verified delivery capacity, dependencies and acceptance criteria; custom proposal.
- "Do you have clients or partners?" Provide only actual verified references or executed agreements; never imply unverified affiliations.
## Follow-up
Track qualification, next action, date, owner, estimate, proposal, accepted scope, invoice and verified payment in private CRM. Reply to requested enquiries promptly during staffed hours. Do not advertise unsupported response guarantees.
