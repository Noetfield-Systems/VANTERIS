# Campaign launch playbook — draft assets, no spend
## Competitive references
SBSB defines industry-specific consulting and scoped hour packages: https://sb-sb.com/services/fin-tech/consulting/. Fintech Harbor presents specialist sector paths: https://www.fintecharbor.com/. Sleek and Osome publish clear formation/accounting scope and prices: https://sleek.com/uk/pricing/ and https://osome.com/uk/register-a-company/price/. VANTERIS uses defined technical/research outputs rather than claiming equivalent regulated capabilities.
## Channels
Organic first: publish approved campaign copy in relevant founder channels; use campaign URLs. Search ads: high-intent service keywords, exact/phrase initially, negative keywords, distinct landing page per service. LinkedIn: founder, expansion, CTO and product audiences; educational posts and scoped offer calls to action. Referrals: use agreed terms and disclosure.
## Suggested test budget — not authorized or spent
CAD 20/day for 7 days, maximum CAD 140, after public landing access, confirmed seller and capacity, review of advertising account eligibility and measurement. Focus one search campaign (roadmap or architecture) initially; expand only on qualified enquiries. No broad retail investment targeting. This is a proposed ceiling, not a performance forecast.
## Measurement
UTM-tagged engagement page → request → persisted lead attribution → qualified → proposal → accepted agreement → invoice → verified paid. No client identities in URLs. Record source and evidence; don't infer revenue from clicks. Compare spend, qualified lead cost, proposal acceptance, customer acquisition cost and verified revenue. Pause irrelevant queries and misleading claims. Existing CRM stores attribution on request/checkout; impression and click data require actual ad platform reporting.
## Creative direction
Editorial navy/ivory/brass; architecture photography licensed in existing asset register; no fake logos or testimonial badges. 1200x628 landscape: headline + single scoped outcome + brand, 1080x1080 square: same hierarchy, 1080x1920 vertical: top headline, readable outcome and CTA. Prefer high contrast, real typography, uncluttered layouts. Use no images of money piles, token returns or regulator marks. Copy variants in campaign-register.csv. Design specs are not rendered image files.
## 10 business-day execution sequence
Day 1-2: verify website and offer delivery capacity; choose contracting seller, actual business contact and availability; qualify 15 existing prospect signals. Day 3-4: publish organic educational posts and individually review referral contacts; record permitted outreach evidence. Day 5: review lead quality and objection patterns. Day 6-10: run approved small test campaign if ready; adjust keyword mix and offer scope; verify invoices and payments. These are operating targets, not completed actions.
## Account requirements
Google announced financial-services verification expansion to US and Canada in early 2027; applicability depends on ad and account classification. https://blog.google/intl/en-ca/company-news/outreach-initiatives/financial-services-verification-united-states-canada/. Follow account notifications and relevant policies. Advertise genuine technical/research services; do not disguise financial activity or make guarantees. Paid campaign launch requires actual advertiser identity and explicit spend limit. Current ads are drafts only.
